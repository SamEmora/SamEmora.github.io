About me

Hard work runs in my family.

I'm Samuel, a Computer Information Systems & Technology student at UT Dallas, based in The Colony, Texas. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I love learning new things.

Where my work ethic comes from

My dad runs a small family business, Colormix Painting. He taught me how to work at a young age: show up on time, do the job right, and treat every customer with respect. I still help him with the business today, and watching him build something of his own is a big part of why I want to start a company someday.

That same mindset carried into my restaurant jobs, where I started as a server at Mi Cocina, moved up to bartender, and now split my time between serving and bartending at Blue Goose, all while going to school full time.
