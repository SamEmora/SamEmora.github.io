About me

Hard work runs in my family.

I'm Samuel, a Computer Information Systems & Technology student at UT Dallas, based in The Colony, Texas. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I love learning new things.

Where my work ethic comes from

My dad runs a small family business, Colormix Painting. He taught me how to work at a young age: show up on time, do the job right, and treat every customer with respect. I've been painting with him since 2018, and watching him build something of his own is a big part of why I want to start a company someday.

Since then I've worked in trades, retail, logistics and hospitality. Each job taught me something different, but the same thread runs through all of them: taking care of customers and helping train the people around me.
